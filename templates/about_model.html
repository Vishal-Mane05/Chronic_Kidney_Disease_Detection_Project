Machine Learning Model

Random Forest Classifier — Clinical Data Prediction

Random Forest is an ensemble of decision trees that votes on the final prediction. It naturally handles feature interactions, is robust to outliers, and outputs well-calibrated probability scores — ideal for clinical classification.

Deep Learning Model

ResNet50 CNN — CT Scan Classification

ResNet50 is a 50-layer deep CNN pre-trained on ImageNet. Its residual skip connections solve the vanishing gradient problem in very deep networks. The top classification layers were fine-tuned to distinguish 4 kidney CT scan categories.
